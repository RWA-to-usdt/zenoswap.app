Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

At ZenoSwap Inc. ("ZenoSwap", "we", "our", "us"), your privacy is important. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account data: name, email, password (hashed), phone number
  • Order data: shipping address, billing address, order history
  • Payment data: card type, last 4 digits (full card numbers are never stored on our servers)
  • Communications: emails, support tickets, chat messages

1.2 Information we collect automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type
  • Pages viewed, time spent on site, referring URL
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Improve our website and product selection
  • Prevent fraud and secure our systems
  • Send marketing emails (only if you opt in — you can unsubscribe any time)

3. Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal data. We only share it with:

  • Payment processors (to process transactions)
  • Shipping carriers (to deliver your order)
  • Service providers (email, analytics — under strict data agreements)
  • Law enforcement (only when legally required)

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, hashed passwords, and PCI-compliant payment processing. However, no system is 100% secure — please keep your password safe and notify us immediately of any suspicious activity.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@zenoswap.app.

6. Cookies

We use cookies for essential site functionality, analytics, and (optionally) marketing. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work correctly.

7. Children's Privacy

ZenoSwap is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes by email or via a prominent notice on our site.
