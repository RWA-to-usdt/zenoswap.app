Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

↩️

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Not happy with your purchase? Return it within 30 days for a full refund — no questions asked.

1. Return Eligibility

You can return an item if:

  • It's within 30 days of delivery
  • The item is in original packaging, unused, with all accessories
  • You have the original order number or receipt

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance"
  • Products damaged by misuse, water, or physical impact
  • Hygiene products (earbuds, opened ear tips)
  • Digital gift cards

3. How to Start a Return

  1. Email us at returns@zenoswap.app with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Get your return label — we'll email you a prepaid shipping label within 1 business day.
  3. Pack and ship — place the item in its original packaging, attach the label, and drop it at any authorized carrier location.
  4. Refund processed — once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund within 5 business days.

4. Refund Timeline

StageTime
Return label issued1 business day
Shipment in transit3–7 business days
Inspection after arrival1–2 business days
Refund to your card5–10 business days

5. Exchanges

Need a different size, color, or model? We offer free exchanges within 30 days. Follow the same return process, and let us know what you'd like instead.

6. Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or stops working within warranty, we'll replace it at no cost — including shipping both ways. Just send a photo of the issue to returns@zenoswap.app.

7. Shipping Costs

  • Defective / wrong item: we cover return shipping
  • Change of mind: free prepaid label (customer keeps original shipping cost)
  • International returns: customer covers return shipping

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank. We resolve 99% of issues within 48 hours. Filing a chargeback before contacting us may delay resolution by 30+ days.
