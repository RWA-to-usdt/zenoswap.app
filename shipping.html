Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: November 17, 2026

🚚

Next-Day Dispatch Worldwide

All orders placed before 2 PM (EST) are processed and shipped out the very next business day. Free shipping on all orders.

1. Processing Time

  • Order cut-off: 2:00 PM EST, Monday – Friday
  • Dispatch: Within 1 business day (Monday – Friday)
  • Weekend orders: Shipped the following Monday
  • Public holidays: Delays may occur during national holidays

2. Shipping Rates

We are proud to offer free worldwide shipping on every order — no minimum purchase required.

3. Tracking Your Order

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number to monitor your shipment every step of the way.

If you haven't received a tracking number within 48 hours of ordering, please contact our support team.

4. Customs & Import Duties

All international orders may be subject to customs clearance procedures and import duties imposed by your country's government. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our shipping fees.

Please check with your local customs office for more information before placing an order.

5. Lost or Delayed Shipments

While we work with reliable carriers, delays can occasionally happen due to weather, customs, or carrier issues. If your shipment is delayed, please allow an additional 3–5 business days before contacting us.

If your package is marked as delivered but not received, please check with neighbors, your building's mailroom, or the local courier facility first.

6. Shipping Restrictions

We currently ship to 28 countries worldwide. If your country is not listed at checkout, please contact our support team to discuss possible arrangements.
