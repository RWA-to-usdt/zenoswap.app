About ZenoSwap

We believe great tech should feel effortless — and look good doing it.

Our Story

ZenoSwap started in 2023 as a small side project by three engineers who were tired of overpriced, overcomplicated tech accessories. We wanted simple products that just work — no gimmicks, no subscriptions, no unnecessary features.

What began as a handful of wireless chargers sold to friends has grown into a global store serving over 12,000 customers across 28 countries. Our mission hasn't changed: deliver premium tech at honest prices, with zero drama.
